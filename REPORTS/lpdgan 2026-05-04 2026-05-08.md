### Artifacts & Datasets

**Datasets on disk:**
- `dataset/LPBlur/` — License Plate Blur dataset. Symlinked from external source. Contains blurry/sharp image pairs for training. Size ~86KB (symlink only, actual data elsewhere on disk)
- `dataset/quan_lp_dataset/` — Quantized License Plate dataset. Created by running plate info generation scripts. ~128KB of content inside, plus a 294MB zip when compressed for transfer
- `data/zips/` — Raw zip files extracted from datasets. Older artifacts from initial data preparation

**Training artifacts:**
- `checkpoints/LPDGAN/checkpoints/LPDGAN/web/` — Generated automatically by training loop. Contains model snapshots saved during training (every N epochs/iters)
- `checkpoints/LPDGAN/loss_log.txt` — Appended to by training script on each checkpoint. Logs generator and discriminator losses.
  ```
  G_GAN: -1.799  G_L1: 0.462  PlateNum_L1: 0.000  D_GAN: 1.038  P_loss: 0.357  D_real: 0.276  D_fake: 1.800  D_s: 1.228
  ```
  Negative G_GAN means generator fooling discriminator well. D_fake ~1.8 means discriminator thinks fakes are close to real.
